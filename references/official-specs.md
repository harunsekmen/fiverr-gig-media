# Official Fiverr specifications

Checked: **2026-10-26**. These notes summarize the linked official pages. Recheck them and the current uploader for new uploads.

## Standalone Gig images

[Creating Gig images and tagging your Gig gallery](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/15863342952977-Creating-Gig-images-and-tagging-your-Gig-gallery) recommends **1280 × 769 px at 72 DPI**, with minimum **712 × 430** and maximum **4000 × 2416**. It discourages stretching, recommends readable, contrasting text, and advises a maximum of 10 words.

This page does not define the numeric safe-area insets used in this project.

## Video and thumbnail selection

[Adding and managing your Gig video](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010451657-Adding-and-managing-your-Gig-video) describes landscape MP4/AVI uploads. After approval, choose a thumbnail by opening **Gig Edit → Gallery → Video → Edit**, pausing on the desired frame, selecting **Set as preview**, and closing the editor.

The preview is a video frame; the page does not publish a 104 px side-buffer rule or a 3:2 artwork safe zone.

## Video canvas and conflicting limits

[Creating a Gig](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010451397-Creating-a-Gig) says the video thumbnail takes priority over Gig images. Its video troubleshooting section specifies **16:9**, **minimum 1280 × 720**, and MP4 with H.264/AAC; its re-encoding advice says to avoid black bars.

The official pages contain conflicting limits: the Gallery section lists 20–60 seconds and under 50 MB, while the video article lists up to 75 seconds and inconsistent file-size wording involving 50 MB and 5 GB. The troubleshooting section also mentions 5 GB. These notes do not resolve that conflict into a universal limit; follow the current upload screen and applicable category requirements.

The reference's 1280 × 714 frame, side buffers, optional text insets, and final 0.3-second cover are project choices, not official requirements. Palette-matched buffers are our design adaptation, not a quoted Fiverr recommendation.
